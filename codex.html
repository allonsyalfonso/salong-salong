Two full custom classes unique to Sina Una. Choose one at character creation (Level 1), the same as any standard PHB class.

Babaylan Abayan Spirit ▾

The spirit-speakers of the islands, custodians of the unseen world. Each Babaylan walks with an ancestral companion, the Abayan, shaping reality through the callings of memory, sacrifice, or wrath.

Source
SinaUna p.164 (full class)
Role
Full custom class (not a subclass)
  • Abayan (Lv 1): An incorporeal ancestral spirit that accompanies you. Acts as spellcasting focus, spell delivery, and origin point for area effects.
  • Spellcasting (Lv 1): Full caster. Draws from the Babaylan spell list (SinaUna p.224+).
  • Spirit Conduit (Lv 3): Channel your Abayan's connection to extend and amplify your magic.
  • Spirit Recovery (Lv 7): Recover expended spell slots through communion with your Abayan.
  • Preternatural Speaker (Lv 10): Deepened link with the spirit realm unlocks rare conversations.
  • Walker Between Worlds (Lv 14): Move between the living realm and the spirit realm at will.
  • Mythic Reverence (Lv 20): Capstone ability that anchors your legend into the world's memory.

Callings (choose one at Lv 3):

  • Call of Clairvoyance (SinaUna p.168): Spirit-seer and fate-reader.
    • Divining Eye & Vessel for the Spirits (Lv 3): Your Abayan becomes a scrying focus; pierce illusions and read omens through it.
    • Clarity of Mind (Lv 6): Heightened perception through your Abayan's sight — advantage on Perception and Investigation checks.
    • Piercing Sight (Lv 11): See through magical concealment and illusions within 30 ft; Truesight through your Abayan's position.
    • Living Memory (Lv 15): Access the Abayan's ancestral memory — ask it one question about the past per long rest; it answers truthfully from its lineage's knowledge.
  • Call of Sacrifice (SinaUna p.168–169): Healer who yields their own vitality to mend others.
    • Undaunted Healer & Absolve Hurt (Lv 3): Spend your own HP to restore HP to a creature you touch; transfer up to your Wis mod in HP per Bonus Action.
    • Yield Vitality (Lv 6): When you heal with a spell, you may additionally grant the target resistance to one damage type until the start of your next turn by expending 5 HP.
    • Hale of Body (Lv 11): Your sacrificial gifts are self-replenishing — regain HP equal to half any HP you spend on Undaunted Healer, once per turn.
    • Banish Affliction (Lv 15): As an action, remove one curse, disease, or condition from a creature by taking 2d10 psychic damage yourself (no save).
  • Call of Wrath (SinaUna p.169): Warrior-mystic who fights alongside the Abayan.
    • Bonus Proficiencies, Guided Strikes & Fury of the Spirits (Lv 3): Gain martial weapon and medium armor proficiency. Your Abayan can deliver weapon attacks through you, adding your Wis mod as bonus damage on hits.
    • Magic Warrior & Guided Strikes Extended (Lv 6): Your weapons count as magical. Guided Strikes can now redirect a miss into a hit once per turn by expending a 1st-level spell slot.
    • Tempered Will (Lv 11): Resistance to psychic damage; advantage on Wis and Cha saving throws.
    • Armor of Ancients (Lv 15): As a Bonus Action, ancestral spirits shroud you in spectral protection for 1 minute — AC increases by 2 and you gain resistance to one damage type of your choice.
Headhunter Hunter's Omen ▾

Relentless trackers bound by omen and ancestry. The Headhunter marks their quarry and does not relent until the hunt is finished, or they themselves become its memory.

Source
SinaUna p.170 (full class)
Role
Full custom class (not a subclass)
  • Focused Hunter (Lv 1): Advantage and bonuses against marked quarry.
  • Conviction (Lv 1): Your resolve to finish the hunt grants persistent bonuses.
  • Rite of the Ancestors (Lv 2): Call on ancestral strength before battle.
  • Fighting Style (Lv 2): Choose from Defense, Distance, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection, Pursuant, or Skirmisher.
  • Hunter's Omen (Lv 3): Subclass choice shapes the nature of your pursuit.
  • Forgiveness for the Slain (Lv 6): Honor the dead you leave behind.
  • Unwavering Hunter (Lv 9): Cannot be stopped once the hunt has begun.
  • Tireless Pursuit (Lv 11): Ignore exhaustion and terrain on the trail.
  • Dread Presence (Lv 13): Your mark inspires fear in those who have wronged your ancestors.
  • Inevitable (Lv 17): Capstone: no quarry escapes you.

Omens (choose one at Lv 3):

  • Omen of Knowledge (SinaUna p.173): Hunt through wisdom and the memory of the dead.
    • Lessons of the Departed (Lv 3): Commune briefly with a creature that died within 24 hours — it answers three questions truthfully before passing on.
    • Memories of War (Lv 3): Learn four slot-abilities you can swap between rests: Assured Mark (auto-hit on first attack against quarry per turn), Cloaked Terror (become invisible to quarry for 1 round after marking), Cycle Breaker (break the quarry's Concentration as a Bonus Action), The Guardian (impose disadvantage on all attacks against one ally per round).
  • Omen of Strife: Hunt through direct confrontation; thrive on taking damage. The harder you are hit, the faster and more precise your pursuit becomes — taking damage grants bonus attacks and strips away your quarry's defenses.
  • Omen of Swiftness: Outpace the quarry; strike before the omen fades. Speed increases, you can Dash as a Bonus Action, and your first attack each turn deals extra damage if you moved at least 10 ft before swinging.

Twelve Sina Una subclasses for the standard 2024 PHB classes. Each is chosen at Level 3. Click a subclass to view its features.

Barbarian
Barbarian Path of the Black River Black River ▾

Bound to the restless current that flows between life and death, these barbarians rage with water in their veins. They strike with the rhythm of the tide, and return to where it began.

Source
SinaUna p.176
  • Isolation of the Soul (Lv 3, Str mod / short rest): On a successful attack while Raging, force a Wis save or the target loses sight beyond 30 ft; your hits against it restore HP equal to your Prof Bonus.
  • Treading Rampage (Lv 6, Reaction): Spend HP to instantly end a condition (Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned). Extends to Petrified/Stunned at Lv 10.
  • Living Tide (Lv 10, 1 / long rest): Cast Dimension Door. You can bring more than one willing creature by spending HP equal to your Str mod per extra passenger.
  • Burial Shroud (Lv 14): When you reduce a creature to 0 HP while Raging, the Black River claims its soul. You gain Temp HP equal to your Barbarian level, and your Rage cannot end until combat ends — no damage needed to sustain it. Additionally, you and allies within 10 ft cannot be Frightened while you Rage.
Bard
Bard College of Siday Folkloric Epics ▾

Keepers of the folkloric epics of Salong-Salong, the Siday bard carries the songs that outlast empires. Their verses bolster the brave and lift the footsteps of heroes past.

Source
SinaUna p.177
  • Noble Fortitude (Lv 3): Gain bonus HP equal to your Bard level (retroactive). Gain proficiency with medium armor and shields. The ancient keepers of the siday endured long roads and longer memories.
  • Folkloric Epics (Lv 3): Learn three Salong-Salong oral epics. Each is a Bardic Inspiration with a special effect when expended — such as granting the ally resistance to a damage type for 1 minute, letting them reroll any one die during that time, or adding your Bardic Inspiration die to their next damage roll.
  • Bolstering Verse (Lv 6): Allies within 30 ft who hold your Bardic Inspiration are immune to the Frightened condition. As a Reaction, when a nearby ally fails a saving throw, you may expend a Bardic Inspiration die to let them immediately reroll it.
  • The Footsteps of Heroes (Lv 14): Once per long rest, invoke the memory of one of Salong-Salong's great siday heroes. For 1 minute, you gain that hero's legendary boon — exceptional speed, a once-per-round legendary action, or immunity to one condition — as their echo walks in your steps.
Cleric
Cleric Volcano Domain Pyroclasm ▾

Servants of the fire that sleeps beneath the islands, these clerics channel the wrath and renewal of the volcanoes. Where they walk, the earth remembers what it once was.

Source
SinaUna p.178
  • Volcanic Calling & Channel Divinity: Pyroclasm (Lv 3): Gain Domain Spells (Burning Hands, Hellish Rebuke / Scorching Ray, Silence / Fireball, Meld into Stone / Fire Shield, Wall of Fire / Flame Strike, Immolation). Pyroclasm Channel Divinity: unleash a 30 ft cone of volcanic fire and ash — creatures inside take Fire + Bludgeoning damage (Dex save for half), and the ground becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute.
  • Revitalize and Renew (Lv 6): After you deal damage with Pyroclasm or a Fire Domain Spell, choose a number of creatures in the blast equal to your Wis modifier — they each regain HP equal to your Cleric level. The same eruption that scours the earth nourishes new growth.
  • Divine Strike (Lv 8): Once per turn when you hit with a melee weapon attack, deal an extra 1d8 Fire damage (increases to 2d8 at Lv 14).
  • Igneous Scion (Lv 17): Gain immunity to Fire damage. Once per turn when you deal Fire damage, the target must succeed on a Con save or gain one level of Exhaustion from the volcanic heat burning within them. While standing on fire-scarred, volcanic, or desert terrain, your Channel Divinity recharges on a Short Rest.
Druid
Druid Circle of Tides Adaptation ▾

Attuned to the ebb and flow of coast and river, the Circle of Tides druid adapts to any terrain like water to its vessel. What shape they take depends on where the tide takes them.

Source
SinaUna p.180
  • Quiet Undercurrent (Lv 3): Gain proficiency in Stealth and Survival. You can move through nonmagical difficult terrain without spending extra movement. In coastal, river, or swamp terrain, tracking you requires a DC check against your passive Stealth.
  • Adaptation (Lv 3): At the start of each of your turns (no action), choose one: Tidal (Swim speed = Walk speed, breathe underwater), Terrestrial (Climb speed = Walk speed, advantage on Str checks), or Aerial (jump distance doubled, Feather Fall at will). One adaptation active at a time.
  • Ebb and Flow (Lv 6): When you hit with a spell or attack, switch your active Adaptation as a free action. Each Adaptation grants a resistance: Tidal → Cold, Terrestrial → Bludgeoning, Aerial → Lightning.
  • Shifting Sands (Lv 10): As a Bonus Action, reshape a 10 ft cube of earth, sand, or water within 30 ft into difficult terrain, a low wall (half cover), or a flowing channel. Creatures in the area make a Str save (DC = spell save DC) or fall Prone.
  • Increased Adaptation (Lv 14): You may hold two Adaptations simultaneously. Shifting Sands affects up to a 20 ft cube. While all three terrain types (water, earth, air) are accessible around you, your Wild Shape CR cap doubles.
Fighter
Fighter Kawal Guardian ▾

Sworn protectors of sitio and kin, the Kawal fighter stands where others fall. Their shield bears the weight of loyalty, and their defense is legend.

Source
SinaUna p.181
  • Steadfast Protector (Lv 3): When an ally within 5 ft is attacked, use your Reaction to give them a +3 bonus to AC against that attack. If the attack still hits, reduce the damage dealt by your Prof Bonus.
  • Guardian Roar (Lv 3, Bonus Action): Let out a challenge cry. Each enemy within 30 ft that can hear you must make a Wis save (DC 8 + Prof Bonus + Str mod) or have Disadvantage on attacks against any target other than you until the start of your next turn. Allies who can hear you gain Temp HP equal to your Prof Bonus.
  • Counter Destruction (Lv 7, Reaction): When an ally within your reach takes damage from a melee attack, make one weapon attack against the attacker. If this attack hits, the original ally takes half the triggering damage instead of full.
  • Resolute Loyalty (Lv 10): Steadfast Protector and Counter Destruction extend to allies within 10 ft (expanded from 5 ft). Choose up to three companions as your sworn charges — they add your Prof Bonus to their AC while you are conscious and within range.
  • Warrior's Legacy (Lv 15): Allies within 30 ft who can see you have advantage on death saving throws. When you use Second Wind, all allies within 30 ft regain HP equal to your Prof Bonus.
  • Peerless Defense (Lv 18): Your sworn charges from Resolute Loyalty cannot be Surprised while you are conscious and nearby. When an attack would reduce a sworn charge within 5 ft to 0 HP, you may use your Reaction to redirect the blow to yourself — you take the damage instead.
Monk
Monk Way of Kaluluwa Schism of Self ▾

Walkers of the boundary between flesh and soul, the Kaluluwa monk lives in two worlds at once. Their discipline is the balance between what the body holds and what the spirit yearns for.

Source
SinaUna p.183
  • Temperament of the Soul (Lv 3): Choose one soul-temperament: Serenity (proficiency in Insight; meditation restores 1 extra Ki on a short rest), Fury (Flurry of Blows pushes targets 5 ft and your strikes bypass resistance to Bludgeoning damage), or Sorrow (when you spend Ki, creatures within 10 ft take psychic damage equal to the Ki spent).
  • Schism of Self (Lv 3, Bonus Action): Your Kaluluwa (spirit-self) acts independently. Direct it to: Attack (unarmed strike from your position), Shield (grant yourself advantage on one save before the start of your next turn), or Scout (gain Blindsight 30 ft until the start of your next turn). One command per turn.
  • Diversion of Magic (Lv 6, Reaction, 2 Ki): When targeted by a magical effect that allows a save, send it back to its source — the caster makes the save instead of you, using your Ki save DC. Can only divert spells of a level equal to or lower than half your Monk level.
  • Refinement of Aspects (Lv 11): Your Kaluluwa can now remain active for up to 1 minute (concentration). While active, it takes its Schism of Self action independently without costing you a Bonus Action — acting on your turn as a free action.
  • Untethered Forms (Lv 17, 1/long rest): Your Kaluluwa fully separates for up to 1 minute. It manifests as a corporeal double within 60 ft, sharing your HP pool and statistics. On your turn, you may take the Attack action for both yourself and the double simultaneously.

Those who have sworn their own blood as the price of protection. Their light shines brightest where they bleed for others.

Source
SinaUna p.185
  • Channel Divinity: My Shield Is For Others (Lv 3): Intercept attacks aimed at your allies, taking the blow yourself.
  • Channel Divinity: Under My Light (Lv 3): Mark an area as yours to defend; enemies within suffer your wrath.
  • Sanguine Light (10 ft) (Lv 7): Radiate a protective aura for 10 ft around you.

For 1 minute, whenever any ally within your aura would drop to 0 HP, they drop to 1 HP instead and you take the overflow damage. During this time, no single source of damage can reduce you below 1 HP — the oath sustains you until the last ally falls.
Ranger
Ranger Mangangayaw Innate Speech ▾

Scouts, speakers, and strikers of the wilds. The Mangangayaw reads the land in languages few still remember, and their march rarely ends.

Source
SinaUna p.186
  • Innate Speech (Lv 3): Choose one approach — Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion. Gain proficiency in that skill (or Expertise if already proficient). You may use Wisdom instead of Charisma for that skill.
  • Striker's March (Lv 3): When you move at least 10 ft before taking the Attack action, each creature you hit on that turn has Disadvantage on their next attack roll and cannot take Reactions until the start of their next turn.
  • Lowering Strikes (Lv 7): Each time you hit your Favored Enemy with a weapon attack, its AC decreases by 1 (maximum −3) until the end of combat. The first AC reduction forces a Str save (DC 8 + Prof Bonus + Dex mod) or the creature's Speed is halved.
  • Undertow (Lv 11): Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, spend one spell slot to invoke the terrain itself — the creature is Grappled (escape DC 8 + Prof Bonus + Str mod) by roots, mud, current, or stone. Grapple persists as long as you are within 30 ft.
  • Eternal March (Lv 15): You need only 4 hours of rest for a Long Rest. Exhaustion from Forced Marches clears after any rest. While tracking a Favored Enemy, you can maintain a forced march indefinitely without accumulating exhaustion levels.
Rogue
Rogue Graverobber Cursed Magic ▾

Those who barter with the restless dead for power stolen from their graves. The curses they carry come with a price, but so does the knowledge.

Source
SinaUna p.188
  • Cursed Magic (Lv 3): Learn one Warlock cantrip (Cha-based). At Lv 9, learn a second cantrip and your Warlock cantrips deal extra damage equal to half your Sneak Attack dice (rounded down) if the target is within Sneak Attack range.
  • Anathema (Lv 3): Choose one curse to infuse your Sneak Attacks:
    • Pestilence: On a Sneak Attack hit, target makes a Con save or becomes Poisoned until end of its next turn.
    • Avarice: On a Sneak Attack hit, target is Distracted — it has Disadvantage on Perception checks and saves until the start of your next turn.
    • Destruction: Your Sneak Attack deals one additional die of its damage type once per turn against creatures already affected by a condition.
  • Eldritch Secrets (Lv 9): Gain two Warlock spell slots (1st level) that recharge on a Short Rest. Learn two spells of 1st or 2nd level from the Warlock spell list, using Cha as your spellcasting ability.
  • Afflict Ruin (Lv 13): When you deal Sneak Attack damage, choose one additional creature within 30 ft of the original target — it also suffers your Anathema curse effect (no attack or save required).
  • Shroud of Desecration (Lv 17, 1/long rest): Enshroud yourself for 1 minute. While active: Sneak Attack deals maximum damage, creatures cursed by Anathema within 30 ft are Frightened of you (Wis save to resist, repeated each turn), and you gain resistance to all damage from any creature currently affected by your curse.
Sorcerer
Sorcerer Diwata Bloodline Spirit Icon ▾

Born with the spark of a diwata's blood, these sorcerers carry the spirit of the land in their veins. Where they walk, the wilderness answers back.

Source
SinaUna p.190
  • Spirit's Cunning (Lv 3): Gain advantage on Insight and Persuasion checks. Once per Short Rest, when a creature makes a Cha-based check against you, you may sense its true intent — you learn exactly what it is trying to accomplish and may choose to grant it advantage, binding the negotiation to honesty.
  • Sorcerous Nature (Lv 3): Choose your diwata's terrain affinity: Forest (illusion/transmutation spells require no material components), Ocean (Cold and water-based spells ignore resistance), Mountain (earth and fire spells ignore resistance), or River (you can cast spells without detection by creatures that cannot see you).   • Icon's Territory (Lv 14): Your Icon radiates a 30 ft aura. Hostile creatures entering or starting their turn in the aura take psychic damage equal to your Cha mod (no save). Friendly creatures in the aura may regain Sorcery Points equal to your Cha mod once per long rest (triggered at the start of their turn).
  • One with Nature (Lv 18): While in your affinity terrain, gain Blindsight 30 ft, cannot be Surprised, and cast your spells without any components. Once per long rest, reshape your affinity terrain within 60 ft as the Move Earth spell.

Wizard Mentala Kawayang Papel ▾

Scribes and keepers of kawayang papel, bamboo-paper magic worked as ink and will. Their durability comes from discipline; their power, from the script.

Source
SinaUna p.196
  • Durability (Lv 3): Gain bonus HP equal to your Int modifier at every Wizard level (retroactive). While not wearing armor, your AC = 10 + Dex mod + Int mod. You may also wear light armor without losing this unarmored defense.
  • Shared Scrolls (Lv 3): Craft a kawayang papel scroll in 10 minutes — inscribe any spell you know of Lv 3 or lower. Any creature may activate it with a DC 10 Arcana check (regardless of class). You may have active Shared Scrolls equal to your Int modifier.
  • Scribe's Malediction (Lv 6): Embed a curse in any Shared Scroll. If a non-ally opens it without your permission, they take 2d8 psychic damage and are Frightened of you for 1 minute (Wis save DC = spell save DC for half damage and no fear).
  • Bendable Magic (Lv 10): When you cast a spell of 1st level or higher, modify it in one way for free (1/turn): extend its range by 30 ft, increase its area (e.g. 20 ft radius → 30 ft), or change its damage type to Fire, Cold, Lightning, or Force.
  • Primordial Conduit (Lv 14, 1/long rest): Speak a word of the first language — older than any script, older than kawayang papel itself. Choose one effect: (a) cast any spell in your spellbook without expending a slot; (b) restore all expended spell slots of Lv 4 or lower; or (c) force all creatures within 60 ft to make a Wis save (DC = spell save DC) or be Stunned for 1 round — the syllable is too ancient for mortal minds to hold.
