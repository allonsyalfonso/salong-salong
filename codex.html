The nine playable races of Sina Una. Click a race to view its full traits.

The most common folk of the archipelago, found in every sitio and barangay. They build, trade, and endure where stranger peoples pass through.

Balat-Kayo Blessing of Haliya ▾

Broad-shouldered and long-lived, the Balat-Kayo carry Haliya's strength in their bones. Where they walk, the old moon-rituals are still remembered.

Size · Speed
Medium · 30 ft
Languages
Common, Sylvan
Senses
Darkvision 120 ft
Lifespan
Matures late teens, lives ~80 years · 6-7 ft tall
  • Adrenaline Rush (Bonus Action, PB / rest):Dash as a bonus action; gain Temp HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  • Relentless Endurance (1 / long rest):When reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, drop to 1 HP instead.
  • Blessing of Haliya:Know Alarm, Detect Poison and Disease, and Identify. Cast each once per long rest as a ritual. Int / Wis / Cha caster.
  • Haliya Ritual (Lv 3):Learn one additional 2nd-level-or-lower ritual; 1 / long rest.
  • Haliya Ritual (Lv 5):Learn one additional 3rd-level-or-lower ritual; 1 / long rest.

Volcano Dwarf Molten Armor ▾

Forged near the magma veins of Bulkang Betis and Kamuning, the Volcano Dwarves wear heat like other folk wear cloth. Their lungs do not flinch at smoke, nor their hands at hot steel.

Size · Speed
Medium · 30 ft
Languages
Common + 2 of choice
Senses
Darkvision 120 ft
Resistances
Fire, Poison · Advantage vs Poisoned condition
Lifespan
Matures early 20s, lives ~350 years · 4-5 ft tall
  • Dwarven Toughness:HP maximum +1, +1 again each level gained.
  • Dwarven Resilience:Resistance to Poison; Advantage on saves to avoid or end Poisoned.
  • Fire Resistance:Resistance to Fire damage.
  • Molten Armor (Bonus Action, 1 / long rest):Gain Temp HP equal to your level. While those Temp HP remain, any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes Fire damage equal to your level. Lasts 1 hour.

Higante Ancestries ▾

Towering descendants of the old giants, the Higante carry a shard of their ancestor's nature in their blood. Some are calm as clouds, some burning as fire.

Size · Speed
Medium · 35 ft (fastest of the eight)
Languages
Common + 2 of choice
Saves
Advantage on ability checks to end the Grappled condition
Carrying
×2 normal (Powerful Build)
Lifespan
Matures late teens, lives ~80 years · 7-8 ft tall
  • Powerful Build:Count as one size larger for carrying capacity.
  • Large Form (Lv 5) (Bonus Action, 1 / long rest):Grow to Large for 10 minutes. While Large: Advantage on Strength checks, Speed +10 ft.

Giant Ancestry (pick one at character creation):

  • Cloud Higante:Cloud's Jaunt (Bonus Action, PB / long rest): Teleport up to 30 ft to a space you can see.
  • Fire Higante:Fire's Burn (PB / long rest): When you hit with an attack, deal +1d10 Fire damage.
  • Frost Higante:Frost's Chill (PB / long rest): When you hit with an attack, deal +1d6 Cold and reduce target Speed by 10 ft.
  • Hill Higante:Hill's Tumble (PB / long rest): When you hit a Large or smaller creature, give it Prone.
  • Stone Higante:Stone's Endurance (Reaction, PB / long rest): Reduce damage taken by 1d12 + Con mod.
  • Storm Higante:Storm's Thunder (Reaction, PB / long rest): When damaged by a creature within 60 ft, deal 1d8 Thunder to it.

Magani Warrior

Those who take up arms for the sitio. Not all sought the role, some were chosen by circumstance and could not put the blade down.

Babaylan Spirit-Speaker

Mediators between the living and the spirit realm. They heal, curse, and speak with the dead. Their authority is older than the datus.

Mentala Paper Mage

Practitioners of kawayang papel, magic worked through bamboo paper, capable of transferring memory, power, and will between people.

Manggagamot Healer

Skilled in herb, root, and ritual. Some heal bodies. Some heal curses. Some heal things that medicine has no name for.
