Isinalin · Localized Backgrounds

Twelve standard backgrounds as they are known in the islands. The flavor is ours; the mechanics are the standard 2024 ones, unchanged. Use the rules for the background named in brackets.

Timawa (Soldier)Savage Attacker▾

You are timawa, sworn to a datu. You defend, you enforce what has been decided, and in return you hold a share of your datu's patronage and a place in the order of things. You came by your weapons honestly and you have used them. The oath is not ceremony, it is the arrangement your whole standing rests upon.

Origin Feat
Savage Attacker
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Soldier background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.185). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Maginoo (Noble)Skilled▾

You were born maginoo, into the lineage a settlement measures itself by. Your kin sit in the torogan, raised above the ground on its great pillars, and the elder council listens when you speak. You learned the bow that is owed to a datu, and the exact courtesy owed by everyone standing below one. Whatever else you lose, the name stays.

Origin Feat
Skilled
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Noble background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.183). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Sirot (Criminal)Alert▾

A fine was set against you that no amount of coin or trade could ever cover. That is how it is done in the islands: the datu weighs the crime, names the price, and the price is meant to be beyond you. So you became sirot, your debt worked off in service rather than settled in silver. You know exactly what your freedom costs, down to the day.

Origin Feat
Alert

Tanod (Guard)Alert▾

You stood the watch. Every settlement worth the name keeps a bantayan raised above the treeline, and somebody has to be in it while the rest of the village sleeps. You learned the difference between a fishing boat and a raiding boat at distance, and the difference between a dog barking at nothing and a dog barking at an aswang. Nothing came ashore on your nights.

Alagad (Acolyte)Magic Initiate (Cleric)▾

You served at the side of a babaylan, an attendant to one who speaks with the spirits. Yours were the small and constant duties: keeping the spirit house swept, laying out betel nut and wine at the altar, holding the bowl while the offerings were made. You learned the shape of every rite by repetition long before you understood its meaning, and in time the spirits grew used to your presence as well.

You worked ground that had to be asked before it could be broken. Rice in the wet season, taro and sago when the rains failed, and an offering left at the field's edge every time either one came in. Farming in the islands is a bargain struck with whatever lives in the soil, and you held up your end of it for years. You can read a sky, a tide and a stubborn carabao equally well.

You are one of the few who commits things to a surface that outlasts the telling. Most law here lives in memory, handed down and argued over between generations, and there are those who think writing it down is a kind of distrust. Mga Kakayahan · Setting Feats

Five feats unique to Sina Una. All are General feats: the prerequisite is level 4 plus whatever else is listed.

Adept ConduitSpirit Dice▾

You have petitioned the unseen spirits and, with patient offerings and communion, learnt to channel their power through yourself.

Prerequisite
Level 4 and the ability to cast at least one spell
  • Ability Score Increase: Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • Borrowed Cantrip: You learn one cantrip from the babaylan spell list. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
  • Spirit Dice: You gain two Spirit Dice (d4s), which add to any Spirit Dice you have from another source. When you cast a spell, you can expend them on the conduit options of the babaylan's Spirit Conduit feature. You regain all expended Spirit Dice on a Long Rest.
CannoneerIgnore Loading▾

Through extensive practice, you have mastered the firepower of the islands' cannons.

Prerequisite
Level 4 and proficiency with lantakas or astinggals
  • Ignore Loading: You ignore the loading property of lantakas and astinggals.
  • Firing in Melee: Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature does not impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls.
  • Enhanced Critical: You can roll one additional weapon damage die when determining the extra damage for a critical hit with a lantaka or an astinggal.
  • House note: As printed, this feat covers lantakas only. The owner widened it to the astinggal so both firearms have feat support. Book feat, widened by ruling.
Headhunting MasterBunang Master▾

The bunang answers to you in both of its aspects, blade and point alike.

Prerequisite
Level 4
  • Bunang Training: You gain proficiency with the bunang if you did not have it already, and you can ignore the weapon's two-handed property.
  • Pointed Side: When you use your Bonus Action to attack with the pointed side of the bunang, you add your ability modifier to the damage of the attack.
Oceanborne WarriorSwim Speed▾

You have become an expert in pursuing opponents through, and even beneath, treacherous waters.

Prerequisite
Level 4
  • Waterborne: You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and fighting underwater does not impose disadvantage on your weapon attack rolls.
Unrelenting HunterEnvenom▾

You have perfected the art of hunting creatures across the islands.

Prerequisite
Level 4
  • Ability Score Increase: Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • Hunter's Kit: You gain proficiency with a hunter's kit.
  • Envenom: As a free action on your turn, you can apply a substance (poison, saltwater and the like) to one slashing or piercing melee weapon you are wielding, or to up to three pieces of ammunition.
