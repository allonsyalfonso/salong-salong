Aklatan ng Mundo

Races, backgrounds and feats for making a character in Salong-Salong.

The nine playable races of Sina Una. Click a race to view its full traits.

HumanIslands of Sina Una Versatile ▾

The most common folk of the archipelago, found in every sitio and barangay. They build, trade, and endure where stranger peoples pass through.

  • Size: Small or Medium (choose at character creation)
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Skills: Choose any one skill
  • Lifespan: ~80 years
  • Height: 2-4 ft (Small) or 4-7 ft (Medium)
  • Resourceful: Gain Heroic Inspiration when you finish a Long Rest.
  • Versatile: Gain an Origin feat of your choice at 1st level.
Balat-KayoIslands of Sina Una Blessing of Haliya ▾

Broad-shouldered and long-lived, the Balat-Kayo carry Haliya's strength in their bones. Where they walk, the old moon-rituals are still remembered.

  • Size: Medium
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common, Sylvan
  • Senses: Darkvision 120 ft
  • Lifespan: ~80 years
  • Height: 6-7 ft
  • Weapon Training: You have proficiency with simple and martial weapons.
  • Adrenaline Rush (Bonus Action, PB / rest): Dash as a bonus action; gain Temp HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  • Relentless Endurance (1 / long rest): When reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, drop to 1 HP instead.
  • Blessing of Haliya: Know Alarm, Detect Poison and Disease, and Identify, and cast each once per long rest as a ritual. At Lv 3 you learn one additional ritual spell of 2nd level or lower; at Lv 5, one of 3rd level or lower. Each is castable once per long rest. Int / Wis / Cha caster.
Umalagad DragonbornIslands of Sina Una Ancestral Protection ▾

Scaled in the likeness of ancestral guardians, the Umalagad walk with the presence of those who came before. Most have lived a full prior life, and the weight of it shows in their eyes.

  • Size: Medium
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Senses: Blindsight 30 ft
  • Lifespan: Varies (most have lived a full prior life)
  • Height: 5-7 ft
  • Ancestral Protection: Know Guidance cantrip (at-will). Int / Wis / Cha caster.
  • Sanctuary (Lv 3): Cast Sanctuary once per long rest.
  • Warding Bond (Lv 5): Cast Warding Bond once per long rest.
Volcano DwarfIslands of Sina Una Molten Armor ▾

Forged near the magma veins of Bulkang Betis and Kamuning, the Volcano Dwarves wear heat like other folk wear cloth. Their lungs do not flinch at smoke, nor their hands at hot steel.

  • Size: Medium
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Senses: Darkvision 120 ft
  • Resistances: Fire, Poison · Advantage vs Poisoned condition
  • Lifespan: ~350 years
  • Height: 4-5 ft
  • Dwarven Toughness: HP maximum +1, +1 again each level gained.
  • Dwarven Resilience: Resistance to Poison; Advantage on saves to avoid or end Poisoned.
  • Fire Resistance: Resistance to Fire damage.
  • Molten Armor (Bonus Action, 1 / long rest): Gain Temp HP equal to your level. While those Temp HP remain, any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes Fire damage equal to your level. Lasts 1 hour.
Balete ElfIslands of Sina Una Verdant Intuition ▾

Long-lived and forest-bound, the Balete Elves are tied to the great Balete trees of the highlands. Few remain after the wars. Dapithapon was one of the last.

  • Size: Medium
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Senses: Darkvision 60 ft
  • Saves: Advantage vs Charmed condition
  • Skills: Choose one: Insight, Perception, or Survival
  • Lifespan: ~750 years
  • Height: 5-6 ft
  • Elven Lineage: Advantage on saves to avoid / end Charmed.
  • Trance: Don't need sleep; immune to magical sleep; Long Rest completes in 4 hours of trance-meditation.
  • Verdant Intuition: Add d4 to any Int (Nature) or Wis (Survival) check.
  • Spirit of Nature: Know Druidcraft cantrip (at-will). Int / Wis / Cha caster.
  • Entangle (Lv 3): Cast once per long rest.
  • Barkskin (Lv 5): Cast once per long rest.
Gold GnomeIslands of Sina Una Gilded Gifts ▾

Small, clever, and gilded with a touch that turns craft into small miracles. The Gold Gnomes bless rather than hoard, and a bracelet of their making is prized across every sitio.

  • Size: Small
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Senses: Darkvision 60 ft
  • Saves: Advantage on Int, Wis, and Cha saves (Gnomish Cunning)
  • Skills: Perception (granted)
  • Lifespan: ~425 years
  • Height: 3-4 ft
  • Resistance cantrip (at-will). Int / Wis / Cha caster.
  • Gilded Gifts (1 / long rest, during a Long Rest): Gild one nonmagical item for 24 hours or until your next Long Rest, whichever comes first. Choose one:
    • Gilded Armor: next wearer gains Temp HP equal to 5 + your Cha modifier.
    • Gilded Tools: user may use your Prof Bonus + Cha modifier instead of theirs.
    • Gilded Weapon: counts as magical; first successful attack is an automatic critical hit.
Mangrove HalflingIslands of Sina Una Natural Craftsmanship ▾

Quiet and quick-handed, the Mangrove Halflings grew up threading between roots and reeds. Nothing stays broken long around them.

  • Size: Small
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Lifespan: ~150 years
  • Height: 2-3 ft
  • Brave: You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
  • Halfling Nimbleness: Move through the space of any larger creature (can't stop there).
  • Luck: Reroll a natural 1 on any D20 Test; use the new roll.
  • Naturally Stealthy: Take the Hide action when obscured only by a creature at least one size larger.
  • Natural Craftsmanship: Know the Mending cantrip (at-will, Int).
  • Quick Thinker: Take the Help action as a Bonus Action.
Carabao TieflingIslands of Sina Una Immovable ▾

Horned and broad-backed, the Carabao Tieflings are stubborn as the beast they are named for. They do not move unless they decide to.

  • Size: Small or Medium (player choice)
  • Speed: 30 ft
  • Languages: Common + 1 of choice
  • Senses: Darkvision 60 ft
  • Saves: Advantage vs Prone / Pushed / Grappled / Restrained
  • Skills: Athletics (granted)
  • Lifespan: ~80 years
  • Height: 3-4 ft (S) or 4-7 ft (M)
  • Otherworldly Presence: Know Thaumaturgy cantrip (at-will). Int / Wis / Cha caster.
  • Immovable: Advantage on saves vs Prone / Pushed / Grappled / Restrained.
  • Legacy of Might (Str mod uses / long rest, min 1): On a successful melee weapon attack, deal the maximum possible damage instead of rolling.
  • Carabao's Heroism (Lv 3): Cast Heroism without a spell slot, 1 / long rest.
HiganteIslands of Sina Una Ancestries ▾

Towering descendants of the old giants, the Higante carry a shard of their ancestor's nature in their blood. Some are calm as clouds, some burning as fire.

  • Size: Medium
  • Speed: 35 ft (fastest of the eight)
  • Languages: Common + 2 of choice
  • Saves: Advantage on ability checks to end the Grappled condition
  • Carrying: ×2 normal (Powerful Build)
  • Lifespan: ~80 years
  • Height: 7-8 ft
  • Powerful Build: Count as one size larger for carrying capacity.
  • Large Form (Lv 5) (Bonus Action, 1 / long rest): Grow to Large for 10 minutes. While Large: Advantage on Strength checks, Speed +10 ft.

Giant Ancestry (pick one at character creation):

  • Cloud Higante: Cloud's Jaunt (Bonus Action, PB / long rest): Teleport up to 30 ft to a space you can see.
  • Fire Higante: Fire's Burn (PB / long rest): When you hit with an attack, deal +1d10 Fire damage.
  • Frost Higante: Frost's Chill (PB / long rest): When you hit a target with an attack roll and deal damage, you can also deal 1d6 Cold damage to it and reduce its Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn.
  • Hill Higante: Hill's Tumble (PB / long rest): When you hit a Large or smaller creature, give it Prone.
  • Stone Higante: Stone's Endurance (Reaction, PB / long rest): Reduce damage taken by 1d12 + Con mod.
  • Storm Higante: Storm's Thunder (Reaction, PB / long rest): When damaged by a creature within 60 ft, deal 1d8 Thunder to it.

Sina Una Feats

Adept ConduitIslands of Sina UnaSpirit Dice▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 4 and the ability to cast at least one spell

You have petitioned the unseen spirits and, with patient offerings and communion, learnt to channel their power through yourself.

  • Ability Score Increase: Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • Borrowed Cantrip: You learn one cantrip from the babaylan spell list. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
  • Spirit Dice: You gain two Spirit Dice (d4s), which add to any Spirit Dice you have from another source. When you cast a spell, you can expend them on the conduit options of the babaylan's Spirit Conduit feature. You regain all expended Spirit Dice on a Long Rest.
CannoneerIslands of Sina UnaIgnore Loading▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 4 and proficiency with lantakas or astinggals

Through extensive practice, you have mastered the firepower of the islands' cannons.

  • Ignore Loading: You ignore the loading property of lantakas and astinggals.
  • Firing in Melee: Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature does not impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls.
  • Enhanced Critical: You can roll one additional weapon damage die when determining the extra damage for a critical hit with a lantaka or an astinggal.
  • House note: As printed, this feat covers lantakas only. The owner widened it to the astinggal so both firearms have feat support. Book feat, widened by ruling.

Oceanborne WarriorIslands of Sina UnaSwim Speed▾

You have become an expert in pursuing opponents through, and even beneath, treacherous waters.

  • Waterborne: You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and fighting underwater does not impose disadvantage on your weapon attack rolls.
Unrelenting HunterIslands of Sina UnaEnvenom▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 4

You have perfected the art of hunting creatures across the islands.

  • Ability Score Increase: Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

Origin Feats

AlertSRD 5.2Origin▾

You gain the following benefits.

Initiative Proficiency. When you roll Initiative, you can add your Proficiency Bonus to the roll.

Initiative Swap. Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with the Initiative of one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally has the Incapacitated condition.

Magic InitiateSRD 5.2Origin▾

You gain the following benefits.

Two Cantrips. You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Cleric, Druid, or Wizard spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).

Level 1 Spell. Choose a level 1 spell from the same list you selected for this feat's cantrips. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have.

Spell Change. Whenever you gain a new level, you can replace one of the spells you chose for this feat with a different spell of the same level from the chosen spell list.

Repeatable. You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different spell list each time.

Savage AttackerSRD 5.2Origin▾

You've trained to deal particularly damaging strikes. Once per turn when you hit a target with a weapon, you can roll the weapon's damage dice twice and use either roll against the target.

SkilledSRD 5.2Origin▾

You gain proficiency in any combination of three skills or tools of your choice.

Repeatable. You can take this feat more than once.

General Feats

Ability Score ImprovementSRD 5.2General▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 4+

Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. This feat can't increase an ability score above 20.

Repeatable. You can take this feat more than once.

GrapplerSRD 5.2General▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

You gain the following benefits.

Ability Score Increase. Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

Punch and Grab. When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use both the Damage and the Grapple option. You can use this benefit only once per turn.

Attack Advantage. You have Advantage on attack rolls against a creature Grappled by you.

Fast Wrestler. You don't have to spend extra movement to move a creature Grappled by you if the creature is your size or smaller.

Blink Steps. Immediately after you take the Attack action or the Magic action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.

Boon of FateSRD 5.2Epic Boon▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 19+

You gain the following benefits.

Ability Score Increase. Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

Improve Fate. When you or another creature within 60 feet of you succeeds on or fails a D20 Test, you can roll 2d4 and apply the total rolled as a bonus or penalty to the d20 roll. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you roll Initiative or finish a Short or Long Rest.

Boon of Irresistible OffenseSRD 5.2Epic Boon▾
  • Prerequisite: Level 19+

You gain the following benefits.

Ability Score Increase. Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 30.

Overcome Defenses. The Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage you deal always ignores Resistance.

Overwhelming Strike. When you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll, you can deal extra damage to the target equal to the ability score increased by this feat. The extra damage's type is the same as the attack's type.
