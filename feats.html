Mga Kakayahan

Five feats unique to Sina Una, from Adept Conduit to Unrelenting Hunter.

Mga Kakayahan · Setting Feats

Five feats unique to Sina Una. All are General feats: the prerequisite is level 4 plus whatever else is listed.

Adept ConduitSpirit Dice▾

You have petitioned the unseen spirits and, with patient offerings and communion, learnt to channel their power through yourself.

Prerequisite
Level 4 and the ability to cast at least one spell
  • Ability Score Increase: Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • Borrowed Cantrip: You learn one cantrip from the babaylan spell list. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
  • Spirit Dice: You gain two Spirit Dice (d4s), which add to any Spirit Dice you have from another source. When you cast a spell, you can expend them on the conduit options of the babaylan's Spirit Conduit feature. You regain all expended Spirit Dice on a Long Rest.
CannoneerIgnore Loading▾

Through extensive practice, you have mastered the firepower of the islands' cannons.

Prerequisite
Level 4 and proficiency with lantakas or astinggals
  • Ignore Loading: You ignore the loading property of lantakas and astinggals.
  • Firing in Melee: Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature does not impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls.
  • Enhanced Critical: You can roll one additional weapon damage die when determining the extra damage for a critical hit with a lantaka or an astinggal.

House note: As printed, this feat covers lantakas only. The owner widened it to the astinggal so both firearms have feat support. Book feat, widened by ruling.

Headhunting MasterBunang Master▾

The bunang answers to you in both of its aspects, blade and point alike.

Prerequisite
Level 4
  • Bunang Training: You gain proficiency with the bunang if you did not have it already, and you can ignore the weapon's two-handed property.
  • Pointed Side: When you use your Bonus Action to attack with the pointed side of the bunang, you add your ability modifier to the damage of the attack.
Oceanborne WarriorSwim Speed▾

You have become an expert in pursuing opponents through, and even beneath, treacherous waters.

Prerequisite
Level 4
  • Waterborne: You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and fighting underwater does not impose disadvantage on your weapon attack rolls.
Unrelenting HunterEnvenom▾

You have perfected the art of hunting creatures across the islands.

Prerequisite
Level 4
  • Ability Score Increase: Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • Hunter's Kit: You gain proficiency with a hunter's kit.
  • Envenom: As a free action on your turn, you can apply a substance (poison, saltwater and the like) to one slashing or piercing melee weapon you are wielding, or to up to three pieces of ammunition.
