  for both races and classes; semantically off but works)

### Critical things NOT to change
- Marker top% values (calibrated, see list above)
- Calibration constant `PX_PER_KM = 9.60`
- The Bestiary lorem ipsum content (placeholder is intentional)
- The 21 empty `src=""` portrait placeholders

### CSS specificity gotcha (encountered twice)
Section h2 pseudo-elements (`::before`/`::after` with the gold side-lines) use
`.dark-section .section-container h2::before` (specificity 0,2,2). Any
`display: none` override on `.about-world__title::before` (0,1,1) loses the
cascade. Use `.dark-section .section-container .about-world__title::before`
(0,3,1) to win. Same trick may apply to other custom h2 styles.
