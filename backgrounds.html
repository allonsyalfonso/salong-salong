Isinalin · Localized Backgrounds

Twelve standard backgrounds as they are known in the islands. The flavor is ours; the mechanics are the standard 2024 ones, unchanged. Use the rules for the background named in brackets.

Timawa (Soldier)Savage Attacker▾

You are timawa, sworn to a datu. You defend, you enforce what has been decided, and in return you hold a share of your datu's patronage and a place in the order of things. You came by your weapons honestly and you have used them. The oath is not ceremony, it is the arrangement your whole standing rests upon.

Origin Feat
Savage Attacker
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Soldier background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.185). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Maginoo (Noble)Skilled▾

You were born maginoo, into the lineage a settlement measures itself by. Your kin sit in the torogan, raised above the ground on its great pillars, and the elder council listens when you speak. You learned the bow that is owed to a datu, and the exact courtesy owed by everyone standing below one. Whatever else you lose, the name stays.

Origin Feat
Skilled
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Noble background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.183). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Sirot (Criminal)Alert▾

A fine was set against you that no amount of coin or trade could ever cover. That is how it is done in the islands: the datu weighs the crime, names the price, and the price is meant to be beyond you. So you became sirot, your debt worked off in service rather than settled in silver. You know exactly what your freedom costs, down to the day.

Origin Feat
Alert
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Criminal background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.179). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Tanod (Guard)Alert▾

You stood the watch. Every settlement worth the name keeps a bantayan raised above the treeline, and somebody has to be in it while the rest of the village sleeps. You learned the difference between a fishing boat and a raiding boat at distance, and the difference between a dog barking at nothing and a dog barking at an aswang. Nothing came ashore on your nights.

Origin Feat
Alert
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Guard background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.181). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Alagad (Acolyte)Magic Initiate (Cleric)▾

You served at the side of a babaylan, an attendant to one who speaks with the spirits. Yours were the small and constant duties: keeping the spirit house swept, laying out betel nut and wine at the altar, holding the bowl while the offerings were made. You learned the shape of every rite by repetition long before you understood its meaning, and in time the spirits grew used to your presence as well.

Origin Feat
Magic Initiate (Cleric)
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Acolyte background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.178). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Magsasaka (Farmer)Tough▾

You worked ground that had to be asked before it could be broken. Rice in the wet season, taro and sago when the rains failed, and an offering left at the field's edge every time either one came in. Farming in the islands is a bargain struck with whatever lives in the soil, and you held up your end of it for years. You can read a sky, a tide and a stubborn carabao equally well.

Origin Feat
Tough
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Farmer background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.180). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Gabay (Guide)Magic Initiate (Druid)▾

You know the ways between places. Not the sea lanes, which belong to voyagers, but the land: which ridge is passable after rain, which river crossing has moved, which grove you walk around rather than through. Half of guiding is knowing the route and the other half is knowing whose permission to ask along it. You have never lost anyone who listened to you.

Origin Feat
Magic Initiate (Druid)
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Guide background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.181). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Lagalag (Wayfarer)Lucky▾

You never stayed. Port to port, island to island, sleeping under other people's roofs and moving on before the welcome thinned. You have chewed betel with strangers on six shores and picked up the local courtesies well enough to pass in each. Ask you about anywhere in the islands and you will have been near it, if not through it.

Origin Feat
Lucky
Mechanics
Identical to the standard Wayfarer background (2024 Player’s Handbook, p.185). Ability scores, skills, tools and starting equipment are unchanged, and are not reprinted here.
Mananayaw (Entertainer)Musician▾

When the kulintang starts, the crowd makes room for you. You have danced at harvest feasts and at funerals, for visiting datus and for spirits who were owed an apology. Your body knows the steps for each occasion the way other people know prayers. Give you a gathering of any size and you will have them watching before the first verse is out.

Origin Feat
Musician
Mechanics

Manunulat (Scribe)Skilled▾

You are one of the few who commits things to a surface that outlasts the telling. Most law here lives in memory, handed down and argued over between generations, and there are those who think writing it down is a kind of distrust. You keep the records anyway: debts, boundaries, judgments, the terms of a settlement's agreements. Memory bends, and you have watched it bend towards whoever spoke last.

Origin Feat
Skilled
Mechanics

Mangangatha (Charlatan)Skilled▾

You make things that are almost true. A story polished until it fits the listener, a mark of ownership on a jar that was never owned, a lineage recited with such confidence that nobody thinks to question it. You were never idle-handed about it either, and you can produce a convincing seal or a convincing smile wherever one is wanted. Most of what you have ever owned, you talked your way into.

Origin Feat
Skilled
Mechanics

Ermitanyo (Hermit)Healer▾

You left. There was a spirit house far up the valley that nobody had tended in living memory, and you went to it and stayed. Years of quiet, of offerings made on behalf of a community that was not there, of listening to something that answered slowly if it answered at all. You came back down carrying an understanding you still cannot explain to anyone who did not sit through the silence with you.

Healer
